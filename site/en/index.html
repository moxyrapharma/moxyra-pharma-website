Building today's healthcare solutions for tomorrow's healthcare vision.

Moxyra Pharma is an Egyptian healthcare and beauty company. We develop proprietary brands in cosmeceuticals, skin care, hair care and nutrition & wellness, turning real market needs into distinctive products. Our products are made through qualified manufacturing partners. Expanding toward broader healthcare and pharmaceutical development is a medium to long-term ambition, not a current manufacturing activity.

InnovationConsumer-led formulation design
Quality firstQuality-focused manufacturing through qualified partners
PartnershipLong-term cooperation over one-off transactions

Our vision

To build a trusted Egyptian healthcare and beauty company with a growing portfolio of distinctive brands, expanding across local and international markets while developing toward broader healthcare opportunities.

Our mission

To develop high-quality healthcare and beauty products that answer real consumer and market needs, build sustainable partnerships, and keep expanding our portfolio through innovation and responsible business development.
