About Moxyra Pharma

MOXYRA PHARMA is an Egyptian pharmaceutical and healthcare company driven by a bold strategic vision to develop and manufacture high-performance, integrated, and diversified healthcare solutions.

Grounded in a deep understanding of evolving patient and consumer needs, we engineer advanced and clinically effective formulations designed to bridge real market gaps—delivering premium, accessible treatments that rival international standards.

Our foundation rests on two core pillars:
  • Uncompromised Quality: Full compliance with global GMP standards in pharmaceutical manufacturing.
  • Healthcare Commitment: Earning physician confidence, establishing trusted partnerships with distributors and pharmacies, and placing patient well-being at the heart of everything we do.

At MOXYRA PHARMA, we do not merely manufacture products; we deliver transformative pharmaceutical and therapeutic solutions. We are actively expanding our global footprint into regional and international markets, reinforcing national manufacturing excellence while contributing to a healthier, more sustainable future.

Vision & Mission

Our Vision

To be the driving force of pharmaceutical and therapeutic innovation in the region, and the most trusted national brand redefining healthcare standards while elevating Egyptian manufacturing quality to global markets.

Our Mission

Building a healthier future by developing safe, highly effective pharmaceutical and therapeutic solutions at a fair value—meeting the real expectations of patients and physicians while cultivating sustainable strategic partnerships that strengthen the healthcare ecosystem.

Scope of Work

Our operational model integrates formulation excellence with strategic market expansion, serving as a trusted healthcare partner across the Arab Republic of Egypt and global markets.

Advanced Cosmeceuticals & Dermatology
  • Engineering clinically advanced formulations tailored for dermatologists and comprehensive skincare & haircare needs.
  • Delivering premium, accessible treatments that rival international standards to bridge real market gaps.
Nutraceuticals & Future Human Pharma
  • Developing unique dietary supplements formulated with uncompromised safety and efficacy to promote general health.
  • Executing a long-term strategic vision to expand into human pharmaceutical manufacturing, reinforcing national drug security.
Pharmacy Activation & Retail Alliances
  • Cultivating partnerships with retail pharmacy chains across all governorates of Egypt via Partner Education & Sell-out Support.
  • Expanding distribution through major pharmaceutical networks to maintain prime positioning in prescriptions and recommendations.
Contract Manufacturing, Private Label & Export
  • Providing end-to-end Contract Manufacturing and Private Labeling (OEM) services under clients' brand names.
  • Fostering transparent B2B commercial alliances for mutual profitability, expanding export capabilities globally.
