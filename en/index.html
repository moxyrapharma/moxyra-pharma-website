Moxyra Pharma is an Egyptian healthcare and beauty company established in 2024 and based in Alexandria. We develop proprietary brands in cosmeceuticals, skin care, hair care and nutritional wellness, turning real market needs into distinctive products. Our products are made through qualified external manufacturing partners. Expanding toward broader healthcare and pharmaceutical development is a long-term ambition, not a current manufacturing activity.

Our vision

To build a trusted Egyptian healthcare and beauty company with a growing portfolio of distinctive brands, expanding across local and international markets while developing toward broader healthcare opportunities.

Our mission

To develop high-quality healthcare and beauty products that answer real consumer and market needs, build sustainable partnerships, and keep expanding our portfolio through innovation and responsible business development.
